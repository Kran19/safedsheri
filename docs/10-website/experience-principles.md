# Public Website Experience Principles — Safed Sheri

## 1. Visual & Interaction Philosophy

The public Safed Sheri website must evoke feelings of entering an exclusive, magical night world. Inspired by benchmark interaction quality (such as [https://brand.ivress.co.jp](https://brand.ivress.co.jp)), it uses WebGL/Three.js 3D depth, particle motion, and smooth scrolling storytelling.

## 2. Core Design Guidelines

1. **No Standard Generic Templates:** Avoid basic stacked boxes (`Hero -> About -> Pricing -> Footer`). Make the site feel like a continuous atmospheric journey.
2. **Pristine Lighting & Contrast:** Use luminous whites, deep night-sky gradients, metallic champagne accents, and soft glassmorphism overlays.
3. **Fluid Micro-Animations:** Scroll-bound 3D rotations, smooth text reveals, dynamic lighting transitions, and responsive hover effects.
4. **Clear Operational Messaging:** Despite high visual storytelling, booking steps and policy reminders (e.g. Mandatory White Dress Code, Cash Counter Workflow) must remain prominent, crisp, and unambiguous.
