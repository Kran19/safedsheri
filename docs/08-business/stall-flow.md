# Stall Application Workflow — Safed Sheri

## 1. Application Overview

Vendor stall applications allow commercial partners (Food & Beverage, Fashion, Merchandise, Craft) to apply for physical booth space at Safed Sheri.

## 2. Form & Management Architecture

* **Public Endpoint:** `/participate/stall`
* **Data Fields:** Business Name, Category (Food / Retail / Activity), Contact Name, Phone, Email, Stall Count Requested, Power / Infrastructure Requirements, Description of Merchandise/Menu.
* **Admin Review:** Super Administrators track applications, update review statuses, record internal operational notes, and assign physical stall booth numbers upon approval.
